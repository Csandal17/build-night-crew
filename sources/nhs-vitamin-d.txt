Government advice is that everyone should consider taking a daily vitamin D supplement during the autumn and winter.

[...] should consider taking a daily supplement containing 10 micrograms of vitamin D during the autumn and winter.

Taking too many vitamin D supplements over a long period of time can cause too much calcium to build up in the body [...] This can weaken the bones and damage the kidneys and the heart.

Do not take more than 100 micrograms (4,000 IU) of vitamin D a day as it could be harmful.
